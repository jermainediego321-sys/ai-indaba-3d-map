# Source-to-model notes

The component combines three planning views from the supplied event PDF:

- **Exhibition floorplan:** numbered stands 1-42, Zone 2 entrance and hall arrangement.
- **Hackathon layout:** food trucks, 10 x 20 m catering, 10 x 15 m hackathon and registration.
- **Floorplan overview:** overall relative site placement, main stage (300 pax), networking space, parking, facilities, field area, catering and safety points.

The model uses metre-like scene units for stable navigation, but only dimensions explicitly printed in the PDF should be treated as stated dimensions. All other geometry is proportionally interpreted for wayfinding and presentation.
